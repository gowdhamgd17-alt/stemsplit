AI separation is very clean but not flawless: backing vocals and harmonies count as voice and go to the vocal track, heavy reverb or effects on the voice can leave faint traces in the instrumental, and voice-like instruments (a sax or violin melody) can bleed slightly into the vocals.

Privacy

Your song never leaves your device. It is decoded and processed inside this browser tab, and nothing is sent to any server. Only the AI model is downloaded, from this website, and your browser keeps a copy so later runs start faster. There are no accounts, cookies or analytics. Closing or reloading the tab, or pressing Clear results, discards your audio.

How it works

The song is processed in 3-second sections that overlap slightly so the joins stay clean. Each section becomes a spectrogram that goes through UVR-MDX-NET Voc_FT, a neural network from the Ultimate Vocal Remover project trained to recognise singing voices. The network outputs the vocal part; the instrumental is the original song minus the vocals. It runs with ONNX Runtime Web, on your graphics card (WebGPU) when available. In tests on real recordings, instrument-only music produced a vocal track more than 44 dB quieter than the music (effectively silent), and voice mixed with a full band was separated with about 15 dB signal-to-distortion.
